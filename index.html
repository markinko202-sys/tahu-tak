Macam mana · How it works

Three steps.
One tingkat.

  1. Pass a set. Get 4 out of 5 right and a dish lands in your kitchen. Go 5 for 5 and the odds of a rare one jump.
  2. Keep it or sell it. Market prices change every day. Collect all ten dishes, or cash in when the price is high.
  3. Buy hints. Spend coins on 50:50, Ask Auntie, a clue, freezing the clock or a skip.
